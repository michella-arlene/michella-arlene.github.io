## Who I am

I'm Michella Arlene Wijaya Radika. I'm a Computer Science student at Bina Nusantara University (BINUS) and a Forward Deployed Engineer at WidaTech, where I've worked full-time on-site since July 2026. I'm based in Tangerang, in the Jakarta metropolitan area, Indonesia.

## How I work

I don't just sit and code. As a Forward Deployed Engineer I manage client projects end to end: I meet clients, learn how their business actually runs, turn messy requirements into specs and design decisions, build the product myself, and then deploy and support it in production. I like owning the whole loop, from the first conversation to the go-live.

## Product background

Before WidaTech, I worked as a Product Manager on team projects. On VoiceChess, an accessible chess app for visually impaired players, I was the Product Manager and also worked on the deep learning models. That product mindset is why I care about whether software actually gets adopted, not only whether it works.
